Cabinet

Please see below agenda links and timetable of meetings for the current municipal year.

  •  Meeting arrangements


    • All Meetings commence at 2.00p.m. 
    • Will be held on a HYBRID basis unless otherwise stated.
    • Items of a confidential or exempt nature to be discussed are not open to the public and will be clearly indicated on the agenda.

  •  How to view a meeting

    Meetings of the Vale of Glamorgan Council are livestreamed and recorded, except where Part II matters are wholly or in part being considered. You can watch meetings and recordings on our Youtube channel.

     

    VoG YouTube Channel

     

    Anyone wishing to attend the Civic Offices to access / attend a remote meeting are requested to contact democratic@valeofglamorgan.gov.uk no later than one working day before the date of the meeting.

  •  Committee


    The Cabinet is made up of the Leader of the Council and seven other Councillors. Click below for full details.
