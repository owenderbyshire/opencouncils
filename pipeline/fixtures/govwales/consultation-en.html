Draft Nature Finance Strategy Wales

We are seeking your views on Wales’s first Nature Finance Strategy.

How to respond

Consultation ends:
29 January 2027
Consultation launched:
26 October 2026

Consultation description

The consultation specifically asks you to:

  • Feedback on the proposed conditions, roles, governance and delivery actions for scaling nature finance in Wales.
  • Identify opportunities for organisations to contribute to delivery.
  • Collect evidence and feedback to strengthen the final strategy.

Consultation drop in sessions

We are hosting a number of online drop-in sessions during the consultation period. These sessions provide an informal opportunity to ask questions, discuss the draft strategy, and seek clarification on any aspect of the consultation before submitting a response.

There will be no formal presentation. You can join for all or part of a session. The sessions will take place on:

  • Friday 6 November 2026, 11:30am to 12:30pm
  • Thursday 26 November 2026, 3:00pm to 4:00pm
  • Thursday 17 December 2026, 2:00pm to 3:00pm
  • Tuesday 5 January 2027, 10:00am to 11:00am
  • Monday 25 January 2027, 1:30pm to 2:30pm

If you are interested in joining, please email Nature.Finance@gov.wales  and we will forward you the Teams meeting link.
