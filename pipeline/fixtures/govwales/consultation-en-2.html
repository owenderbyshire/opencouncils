Carbon dioxide stunning of pigs

We are consulting on a proposal to phase out the use of carbon dioxide (CO2) gas to stun pigs in the UK.

How to respond

Consultation ends:
21 December 2026
Consultation launched:
26 October 2026

Consultation description

We are seeking views on proposals to:

  • phase out the use of carbon dioxide (CO2) gas stunning of pigs in slaughterhouses and knacker’s yards in the UK
  • agree an appropriate length of transition period before any proposed ban might come into force

The proposed options include:

  • do nothing
  • ban carbon dioxide (CO2) stunning of pigs  
  • with transition periods considered: 
  • transition in 2 years 
  • transition in 3 years 

This is a joint consultation between the Welsh, UK, Scottish and Northern Ireland governments. It is led by the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Defra).

This consultation is being held on GOV.UK
