Cabinet Portfolios

The role description and person specification of the Leader, Deputy Leader and Cabinet Members can be found in the Councillors Handbook.

The ten Cabinet Portfolios are as follows:

  • Cabinet Member - Adults Wellbeing
  • Cabinet Member - Children, Young People & families
  • Cabinet Member - Community (Job Share)
  • Cabinet Member - Culture, Human Rights & Equalities
  • Cabinet Member - Development
  • Cabinet Member - Economy, Finance and Strategy (Leader)
  • Cabinet Member - Education & Learning
  • Cabinet Member - Environment & Infrastructure
  • Cabinet Member - Investment & Delivery (Joint Deputy Leader)
  • Cabinet Member - Transformation (Joint Deputy Leader)
